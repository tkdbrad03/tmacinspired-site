How this works

Before our first call

Fill out the intake below. Then open the Program tab and read the Module 1 workbook.

Once your intake is in, I'll use the day and time you gave me to set up our first call.

The Program tab

Six modules. Only the one you're working on is open. When you finish it, I release the next one. This isn't me holding things back from you, it's me keeping you from drowning. People who try to do all six at once finish none of them.

Every module has four things: a workbook to read, an audio or video where I walk you through it, questions for you to answer, and a checklist. Do them in that order.

Hit Save my work when you answer the questions. I read your answers before our call, so the more you put there, the further we get in the hour.

The Progress tab

Six milestones, start to open doors. Mark each one off as you complete it. This is the view I want you looking at when you feel like nothing is happening, because it will show you that something is.

The Calls tab

After every session I post the notes and your action steps before the next one. If you forget what we decided, it's there.

Messages

Anything you get stuck on between calls goes there. I answer within two business days. Don't sit on a question for a week waiting for our call, that's a week you lose.

One last thing

You're going to hit a week where you don't do the work. It happens to everyone. Don't disappear on me when it does. Post in Messages and tell me what got in the way, and we'll adjust. The people who finish aren't the ones who never fall behind, they're the ones who say so.
