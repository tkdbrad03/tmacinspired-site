What Is a Tax Deed?

A legal document granting property ownership to a buyer when the original owner fails to pay taxes. The government sells the property at public auction. Issued after years of nonpayment as a last resort for municipalities.

What Is a Tax Lien?

A claim the government places on a property when the owner fails to pay taxes. Investors purchase lien certificates at auction, paying the owed taxes on the homeowner's behalf. The homeowner must repay with interest or the investor can foreclose.

The Key Difference

In a tax deed state, you purchase the property outright. In a tax lien state, you purchase the certificate and earn interest. Florida operates as both, giving you two powerful strategies in your own backyard.
