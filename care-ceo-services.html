The Honest Math

"I Can Pay Someone $2,500 to Do the Paperwork."

You're right, you can. The state just doesn't issue licenses for filled-out forms. The application is about 10% of the work. The other 90% is what a form-filler can't do, and won't be around for when the state writes back with a deadline.
