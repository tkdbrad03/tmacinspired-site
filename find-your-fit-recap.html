You Do Not Always Need Medicaid to Start

Many businesses begin with private-pay clients or insurance voucher programs before pursuing broader Medicaid or Medicare participation. Several of our clients started with the simplest model and minimal requirements, then expanded over time. Start lean, prove the model, then grow.

1

Form Your Entity

Choose your business name, file for your LLC, and get your EIN. Make it real and legal first.

2

Contact Licensing

Identify and contact the licensing body in your state for your chosen model. Every state is different.

3

Secure Funding

Know your startup number and where the first dollars come from. Private pay and vouchers count.
