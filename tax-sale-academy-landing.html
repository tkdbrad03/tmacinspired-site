The Process

How It Works
Step by Step

The app walks you through the complete process from zero knowledge to your first bid and beyond.

1

Set Your Investment Goal

Long-term appreciation, rental income, or land banking. Your goal shapes every decision. Write it down inside the app.

2

Research Your State and County

Use the State Guide to find out if your state is deed, lien, or both. Then locate the county tax office and get on their sale list.

3

Run Your Due Diligence

Use the built-in Due Diligence Checklist to research title, zoning, flood zones, market value, and access before you bid on anything.

4

Track Your Counties and Properties

Log everything in the County Tracker. Sale dates, budgets, properties of interest, and notes. Export to a spreadsheet anytime.

5

Attend, Bid, and Win

Use the Auction Day Checklist to show up prepared. Stick to your budget. Track your bids. Complete the transaction and get your deed.

6

Reinvest and Repeat

Compound your returns into more properties. Build the portfolio. Pass it down. Rinse and repeat.
