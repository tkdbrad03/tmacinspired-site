Investment Paths

Choose the strategy that fits your life, your schedule, and your goals.

House Hacking

Beginner Friendly

Buy a 2–4 unit property using an FHA loan. Live in one unit and rent the others. Your tenants cover your mortgage — and often generate profit from day one.

Why It Fits Nurses

You already understand how to manage high-stress, high-stakes environments. Managing tenants is a skill set you can build quickly. FHA financing means low upfront costs — ideal for nurses who need capital preserved.

Potential Results

$500–$2,000 per month in net offset or profit. One house hack can eliminate your entire housing cost while building equity and cash flow simultaneously.

Shared Living Homes

High Cash Flow

Lease or own a home and rent it by the room to tenants who need affordable, community-based housing. This model generates 2–3x the revenue of traditional single-family renting.

Why It Fits Nurses

Your care background is an asset. You understand community, dignity, and structure — essential for running a quality shared living environment. Many nurses pivot into this from group home ownership experience.

Potential Results

$3,000–$8,000+ per month from a single property with 4–6 rooms. A shared living portfolio of two or three homes can fully replace bedside nursing income.

Tax Liens & Tax Deeds

Creative Acquisition

Purchase government-issued tax lien certificates or acquire properties through tax deed auctions — often at a fraction of market value. This strategy can produce high-yield returns or distressed property acquisitions.

Why It Fits Nurses

You're detail-oriented, systematic, and comfortable with research — skills that translate directly to analyzing lien certificates, tracking redemption timelines, and evaluating county auction properties.

Potential Results

Tax lien certificates offer 8–36% state-mandated interest rates. Tax deed acquisitions can yield properties at 30–70% below market value when combined with a sound exit strategy.

Private Money Lending

Passive Income

Become the bank. Lend your capital to real estate investors and collect interest — secured by the property itself. Private money lending turns your savings into a passive income engine without the responsibilities of property management.

Why It Fits Nurses

You already manage risk every single shift. As a private lender, your job is evaluating borrowers and deals — not managing tenants or contractors. This is one of the most passive plays in real estate, ideal for nurses who want income without added workload.

Potential Results

Private money loans typically earn 8–15% annual interest. A $50,000 loan at 12% generates $500/month in interest-only payments — secured by a real property. No tenants, no toilets, no turnovers.

Mid-Term Rentals (MTR)

The Goldilocks Method

The sweet spot between short-term chaos and long-term low returns. Rent furnished properties for 30–90 day stays to travel nurses, healthcare professionals, and corporate tenants. Premium income, stable occupancy, minimal management.

Why It Fits Nurses

You know exactly what a travel nurse needs — blackout curtains, quiet, safety, proximity to the hospital. That insider knowledge lets you charge a 20–40% premium above standard market rent for a "home away from home" your peers can't replicate.

Potential Results

MTR properties regularly outperform long-term rentals by 20–40% while requiring far less daily management than Airbnb. One well-positioned MTR near a medical center can generate $2,500–$4,500/month.
