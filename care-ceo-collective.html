The 90-Day Promise

By Day 90, you do not just have a plan.
You have five documents that prove you are ready to open.

These are not worksheets. These are the operational assets a licensed facility requires. You will build each one inside the Collective over 90 days.

Deliverable 01

The Care-Specific Launch Manual

A version of the 90-Day Care CEO Operating System tailored for your specific facility type, documenting exactly how your business will function to meet state standards. This is the document an auditor wants to see before your doors open.

Deliverable 02

Your Regulatory Roadmap

A step-by-step checklist of the specific licensing, zoning, and safety requirements for your facility in your state. Fire Marshal. Health Department. Zoning board. Nothing falls through the cracks on your end. The state timeline is theirs. Your preparation is yours.

Deliverable 03

The Referral Engine Blueprint

A documented plan for building relationships with hospital discharge planners, social workers, and local families before your facility opens. The goal is a waitlist before you have residents, not after. This blueprint shows you exactly how to build it.

Deliverable 04

Pro Forma Financials

Clear, objective projections of your startup costs, staffing overhead, and break-even resident count. No more financial guesswork. By Day 90 you know exactly what it costs to open, what it costs to operate, and when you become profitable.

Deliverable 05

Your Launch Timeline

You have reverse-engineered your 90-day execution sprint to move from Application Filed to Ready for Initial Inspection. Every task. Every deadline. Every dependency. Mapped so you are never asking what comes next.
