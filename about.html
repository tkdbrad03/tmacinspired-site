The Woman Behind the Work

She did not just navigate the healthcare system.
She built businesses inside it.

Dr. Tasha “TMac” McCray has spent 32 years in healthcare, navigating compliance at every level to keep her license, run her facilities, and survive every audit. She holds a PhD, MSN-Ed, and RN. She is a Certified ALF Administrator. She has built and owned group homes.

Based in Tampa, Florida, TMac runs multiple enterprises including CompliTrst™, the compliance system she built for the woman trying to open a group home, adult day care, or assisted living facility across all 50 states. She also manages real estate investments and private money lending, and leads The Tee Elite Circle, an exclusive annual membership community for accomplished women.

Three years ago, she discovered golf. Within that time she achieved two holes-in-one, multiple Player of the Month awards, and became an active competitor in leagues across Florida. The game became a living metaphor for her philosophy: mastery through practice, confidence through repetition, and excellence without apology.

Philosophy

The principles behind the world she built.

Consciousness First

Your external world is a reflection of your internal state. Change the assumption, change the reality. This is not spiritual bypassing. It is physics.

No Hustle Culture

Grinding is not a strategy. Building from alignment creates sustainable wealth. Rest is productive. Ease is your birthright.

Execution Over Theory

Ideas without implementation are fantasies. Systems without soul are prisons. We build both: the strategy and the identity that can hold it.

Tech Meets Purpose

Automation and modern tools are not the enemy of authenticity. They are the amplifiers. Use them to scale what matters and eliminate what does not.

Wealth Without Apology

Community Over Competition

The women in this circle do not compete. They collaborate. Scarcity thinking is a cage. Abundance is contagious.
